# Description **WARNING!** Before operating the device, please review the [important considerations for this model](https://wiki.navtelecom.ru/en/home/devices/notes/no_cpu_1).

 Ultra-Compact Basic 2G GPS Tracker With Backup Battery, Bluetooth, and RS-485 Port for Connection of Digital and Wireless Universal Sensors, Fuel Sensors, Temperature, and Humidity Sensors.

- Internal Sensitive Antennas

- Backup Battery

- 2G Modem

- 1 SIM (Nano-SIM)

- USB Connector Type-C

- Overvoltage Protection Up to 200V

- 2 Digital Inputs

- 1 Analog Input (Configured as Digital)

- 1 Digital Output

- RS-485 Interface

- Bluetooth 4.0
